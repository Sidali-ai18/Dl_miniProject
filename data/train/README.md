# Training Data

Place your training images here, organized by emotion.

## Directory Structure

```
train/
  ├── happy/
  │   ├── person1_happy_1.jpg
  │   ├── person1_happy_2.jpg
  │   ├── person2_happy_1.jpg
  │   └── ...
  ├── sad/
  │   ├── person1_sad_1.jpg
  │   └── ...
  ├── neutral/
  │   └── ...
  └── [other emotions]/
      └── ...
```

## Tips

- Use 70-80% of your total images for training
- Take at least 20-30 images per person per emotion
- Vary lighting, angles, and backgrounds
- Ensure faces are clearly visible
- Use consistent naming: `personX_emotion_Y.jpg`

## Supported Emotions

Common emotions to include:
- happy
- sad
- neutral
- angry
- surprised
- fear
- disgust

You can add more or use fewer emotions based on your needs.
